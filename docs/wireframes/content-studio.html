  • Active chemicals: keep the current SDS accessible on every shift.

Why it looks like this

  1. 1
    Timeline as navigation. Every step, its status in words and the model behind it. Click any finished step to reread it.
  2. 2
    Output reads like the article. Headings keep their shape, each carries its grade, and [VERIFY] markers stand out so you check them before approving.

    Decisions in one bar. Approve is primary; reject is set apart on the right and asks for a reason, so it can't be hit by accident.

    Versions (decided). Each step keeps its first draft plus the three most recent versions, so you can always compare against where it started without a long list.

Why it looks like this

  1. 1
    The profile is the deliverable. It mirrors the SITE_PROFILE in your guest-post skill: narrative mode, voice, headings, length, reading level and link habits. Later steps read it, so you approve or correct it first.
  2. 2
    Every page accounted for. You see which pages were read, how (direct or via the fallback), and which were skipped and why.
  3. 3
    Point it at better examples. If the sample missed the site's typical article, add a URL and it's read the same way.
  4. 4
    Polite and lawful by design. Public pages only, robots.txt respected, slow pace, a page cap. Login-only pages are never fetched.
