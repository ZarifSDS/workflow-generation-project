The Search results step collects candidate keywords (the brief's, autocomplete, People also ask, related searches), asks the first platform below that has a key for their monthly searches, difficulty and intent in the brief's region, then keeps or drops each one by the rules further down. Research then picks only from the kept ones. Without a key, the rules still drop off-topic keywords, near-duplicates and excluded terms. Keys are encrypted, never shown again (only their last four characters), and every change is recorded under Reports › Site history.

Rules for keeping a keyword

The brief's own keywords are always kept. A question is kept even without a volume, because it may still be the FAQ's best question. Volume and difficulty apply only where a platform measured them.
