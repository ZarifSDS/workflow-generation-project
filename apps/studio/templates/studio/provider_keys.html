Every model a provider lists for your key (chat models only) is offered in the model lists, paid ones included: a stored key is the go-ahead to use it. Lists are read again when a key is saved, once a day, and when you refresh. Manage models to rename, re-price, hide or restore several at once.

Prices and context windows fill themselves in: from the provider's own list where it gives them (OpenRouter), otherwise from a public reference price list ({% if reference.fetched_at %}read {{ reference.fetched_at|timesince }} ago{% if reference.error %}; last attempt failed: {{ reference.error }}{% endif %}{% else %}read at the next refresh{% endif %}). A price you type under Manage models always wins. "Billed as" decides whether a price becomes a cost: a free-tier or subscription key costs $0 a call and shows the list price for reference. Usage and cost shows what runs have used.
