By section: FKGL per H2, and per H3 when an H2 section is longer than 300 words (short H3s are graded with the ones after them); a grade needs 100 words; references, citations, URLs, tables and notes aren't counted. Sections more than one grade above target are marked. Hard and very hard sentences read 6 and 10 grades above the target on their own; Tune unpacks those first. Complex words (over 7 letters, not among the 5,000 most common words; EHS terms and the brief's keywords exempt) and passive sentences are advice, never a block: a standard, clearer word stays even when it raises the grade. Tune lowers a section by unpacking sentences (never by swapping in shorter words) and saves a new version; a drop of more than 1.5 grades is refused if it leaves the sentences choppy.
